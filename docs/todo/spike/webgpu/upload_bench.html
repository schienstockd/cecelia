3D texture upload — is the cold path bound by rows or by bytes?

Measured: uploading a timepoint costs ~3 µs per row, and total time tracked row count (2.96x) far better than byte count (7.41x) between two real images. The suspicion is that queue.writeTexture repacks every row because bytesPerRow is not a multiple of 256 (1104x2 = 2208, 441x2 = 882). This isolates that: same data, four upload paths, at widths that are and are not 256-aligned. Synthetic data — this measures the transfer, not the store.
