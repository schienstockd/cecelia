Timecourse slider — the one place the browser has no headroom

Rendering a frame is 5.3 ms; fetching a timepoint is ~1.2 s. So a naive slider is unusable and the interesting question is what makes it usable: a VRAM texture cache with a byte budget, LRU eviction, directional prefetch, and coalescing so a dragged slider does not queue a request per pixel. The strip under the slider is the cache — green is resident, amber in flight.
