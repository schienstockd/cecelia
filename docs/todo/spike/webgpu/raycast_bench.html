G2 — WebGPU volumetric raycast, option B (v3)

v1 produced impossible numbers — 4 channels cheaper than 1, 512 steps cheaper than 256, a bigger viewport faster than a smaller one — because each frame was one submit followed by a mapAsync round trip (wall time pinned at exactly 100 ms for all 12 configs), and nothing ever read the render target, so the driver was free to discard the work. v3 fixes both: N frames in ONE submit with timestamps around the whole batch, and a pixel readback after the last pass so the result is provably consumed. Monotonicity gates below decide whether the numbers may be quoted.
